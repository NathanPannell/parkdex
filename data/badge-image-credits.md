# Badge image credits

These locally served photographs are public domain or licensed for reuse under the Creative Commons licenses shown below. Attribution and share-alike obligations remain applicable. Images are resized Commons thumbnails, displayed with a crop to fit; they illustrate species, not necessarily a photograph taken on Vancouver Island. The orca image shows a Southern Resident killer whale near San Juan Island.

Each badge links its photograph and license in the app. Preserve this file and the image manifest when redistributing the assets.
