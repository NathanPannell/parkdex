# Describe access surveys by what was verified on foot

Recorded: before 2026-10-07. Applies to: park access notes, route copy and product experiment advice.

## What went wrong

An in-person walking survey was described as a "drive-by" survey because the surveyor drove to the area. Separately, advice on product experiments treated a handoff to an external map app as if it were the whole in-app journey.

## Rule

- Separate the travel mode used to reach an area from what was checked on foot. Someone who drives to a park and walks from a transit stop to an entrance has field-checked that walk. List bus schedules and an end-to-end bus ride as separately verified facts.
- Keep the intended journey intact: choose a reachable park, understand the route and the field-checked final walk, arrive, and collect the visit. An external map or transit link can support this, but it is not a complete version of it.

## How to check

Each access claim in copy names how it was verified: walked, ridden or taken from a schedule.
