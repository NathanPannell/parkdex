## Provincewide coverage and scalable sourcing

At this review, the generated places data contains 1,030 places and the app has 130 verified photo records. Coverage is 63 of 693 provincial parks, 23 of 293 regional parks, 7 of 7 national parks, and 37 of 37 major islands. This pass adds photos for all 13 newly curated major islands. Existing park photo coverage remains partial for provincial and regional parks; this task did not download bulk candidate images or add unverified matches.

Commons is a useful candidate source at this scale, but its search result alone is not evidence that an image depicts a particular park, and Commons says uploaders' copyright statements are not guaranteed. A repeatable discovery pass can use the official [MediaWiki Search API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API%3ASearch) to find exact park names, known alternate names, and feature names, then use [Imageinfo](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API%3AImageinfo) with `extmetadata`, original dimensions, and source URLs to inspect each candidate. Before adding a photo, verify the depicted location from its file description/caption and categories, visually inspect it, check its license and restrictions on the file page, and record the creator, license name and URL, Commons file URL, original URL, and location evidence. Keep candidates with CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or verified public-domain status; exclude noncommercial, no-derivatives, unclear, or restricted files. Review CC BY-SA attribution and share-alike requirements under [Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons%3AReusing_content_outside_Wikimedia/en). Store accepted files locally as WebP derivatives and retain the original attribution in the catalogue.

For efficient coverage growth, run candidate discovery in batches by authority and region, rank exact-name and on-site-feature matches above broad regional scenery, then manually review the highest-confidence candidates. Treat each image as a separately licensed work and preserve the catalogue's current file-size budgets. The present counts are the starting baseline for that backlog, not a claim that Commons contains one reliable image for every park.
