## Coverage and result

- Openverse commercial-license query: **43/43 attempted successfully**; 32 returned zero results and 11 returned 31 results in total. Only the three accepted files combined exact-place evidence with a compatible license and useful park imagery.

The seven not separately searched on Flickr were Kennedy River Bog, Muqqiwn/Brooks Peninsula, Nimpkish Lake, Sydney Inlet, Tranquil Creek, Weymer Creek, and White Ridge; each still received its Openverse query and had the earlier Commons exact-name pass.
- Verified and downloaded: **3** — Mansons Landing, Morden Colliery Historic, and Spider Lake.
- Remaining after this bounded pass: **40**. These are missing, not proved impossible.

## Limits

Openverse returned only 31 total results across the 43 searches and most were unrelated keyword matches. Search indexing and absent file-level licenses remain the main limits. Every withheld lead must retain missing status until both exact park placement and commercial redistribution rights are verified; a caption proves location, while a license page proves reuse permission.
