# Home surface brief

Mode: Operate.

The visitor succeeds by finding a verified park or island, checking it off, and seeing collection progress persist. Mobile use is primary, with map readability, thumb reach, outdoor contrast, and graceful weak-connectivity behavior prioritized. The first viewport proves the core mechanism by showing clustered collectible markers, live progress, and an immediately reachable collection control. The pinned visual direction is the user's game-like, cartoony map interpreted as a premium Pacific Northwest field guide; factual content and source provenance remain visually clear.
